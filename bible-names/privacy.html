Privacy Policy – Holy Names

Holy Names (Bible names in Hindi) · Android app in.holynames.bible and website · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Summary

Holy Names does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store or sell any personal information. The names, family trees, maps and other content are bundled with the app and work on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, contacts, location or files. Any favourites, quiz scores or settings are stored only on your device (local storage) and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Maps and links

Map backgrounds are loaded from Esri map tile servers when you open a map, which requires an internet connection; those servers receive standard request data such as your IP address. Links to YouTube open in the YouTube app or your browser and are governed by their own policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience Bible reference and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
